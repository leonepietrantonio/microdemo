# Pezzo brief

Minimum supporting objects: 3

## Creative ambition

Create a luxury editorial campaign with a specific, art-directed world around one unmistakable hero product. Each image should feel conceived, styled and photographed for this brand. Richness comes from meaningful relationships between objects, materials and space, with a clear visual hierarchy.

## Intersect with the brand brief

Start from the brand's observed design language: silhouette, craftsmanship, palette, cultural references, attitude, campaign settings and material vocabulary. Turn those observations into a scene idea. Explain how the scene belongs to this particular brand and how Pezzo elevates it. Do not impose the same set or prop collection on every brand.

Use the brand brief to determine identity and relevance. Use this Pezzo brief to determine the level of composition, styling and editorial ambition. Keep observed brand facts distinct from creative interpretation.

## Build a rich scene

Give the hero at least the minimum number of distinct supporting objects specified above. Select objects with a purpose: a cultural cue, a material counterpoint, a useful scale reference, a shape echo, a narrative clue or an elegant means of elevation. Name each object's material, placement and role. Floors, walls, backdrops and light effects do not count as supporting objects.

Compose foreground, middle ground and background with intentional depth, overlapping forms and different heights. Use crafted arrangements, unexpected but coherent juxtapositions and a deliberate camera position. An environment alone is insufficient: the scene needs objects and relationships. A bare ground plane and wall, a solitary pedestal, or a product floating in an empty room does not satisfy this brief.

Rich does not mean cluttered. Give the protagonist room to read. Supportive objects must direct attention toward it rather than compete with its silhouette, label, finish or distinctive construction. Avoid generic luxury shorthand and arbitrary flowers, books, fabric or marble unless they belong to the brand's world and the scene concept.

## Elevate the product

Preserve the reference product's exact shape, color, construction, proportions, finish and genuine branding. Do not blend variants, invent details or conceal important features. Choose a viewpoint that reveals what makes this product desirable. Use a sculptural arrangement, a thoughtful support, scale, depth or material contrast to make it the focal point.

For fashion, retain the same environmental richness and product hierarchy. Casting, pose, wardrobe and interactions with the set should express the brand's attitude. The featured garment or accessory remains identifiable and central to the image.

## Material and color direction

Describe tactile, specific materials and a considered palette drawn from the brand. Combine surfaces with different visual qualities: polished and porous, translucent and opaque, soft and rigid, aged and precise. Use restraint in the palette and richness in the arrangement. Build believable scale, physical support and contact between objects.

## Gobo lighting and selective shadow

Rainfrog's selected Line Gobo No Back style must remain active in every visual. Gobo lighting on the hero subject is mandatory: the subject must sit within the illuminated area and remain the clearest, most readable element of the image, while selected parts of the surrounding composition fall into shadow.

Compose the scene around this relationship. Place supporting objects across illuminated, partially shaded and shadowed areas to create depth and guide attention toward the hero. Allow portions of the foreground, set and background to recede into deep shadow, while retaining enough material detail and spatial information to make the environment feel rich and intentional.

Preserve the style's sculptural, selective illumination and broad, soft, feathered transitions. The gobo effect must visibly shape the illumination on the subject without obscuring its defining silhouette, genuine branding, finish or distinctive construction. Avoid uniform illumination across the subject or the entire scene.

Do not request visible beams, projected graphic patterns, stripes, slits, venetian-blind shadows or hard-edged bands of light. The native style supplies the gobo treatment; scene direction should establish the hero's position in light and the surrounding composition's relationship to shadow.

The text prompt may specify daylight, warm or cool color temperature, the setting and the background atmosphere. Write scene direction separately from the lighting recipe. Keep the native preset and selected style node active. Rainfrog resolves its lighting recipe internally when the wired image-generator node is executed. Never copy style recipe text into the scene description.

“No Back” leaves the background open to art direction. Choose a rich background and setting that support the story, with intentional areas of light and shadow.

## Review before generation

Check brand specificity, at least three meaningful supporting objects, clear product hierarchy, faithful product identity and considered depth. Confirm that the selected gobo style remains active, the hero sits in light with its defining features readable, and parts of the surrounding composition remain in shadow. The finished scene should feel like a luxury editorial campaign rather than a catalog photograph.

## Write each visual brief

Give each shot a distinct editorial concept and a concrete composition: hero product and viewpoint; environment; supporting objects with materials and spatial relationships; palette; foreground/background depth; camera framing; optional light temperature. Describe an image a stylist and photographer could stage. Avoid relying on vague words such as premium or beautiful in place of concrete decisions.

Across three visuals, vary the narrative, object relationships, material contrasts and camera angle while maintaining a coherent brand identity. Write actual scene direction as the scene brief. The text input contains scene direction only; the native image generator resolves the wired style, products and other inputs internally.

## Review before generation

Check brand specificity, meaningful supporting objects, clear product hierarchy, faithful product identity, considered depth and compatibility with the selected lighting style. The finished scene should feel like a luxury editorial campaign rather than a catalog photograph.
